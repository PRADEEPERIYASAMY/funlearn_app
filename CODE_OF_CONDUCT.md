# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Everyone participating in FunlearnV2 — through issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other channel — is expected to maintain a respectful and constructive environment.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in all communication.
- Give and receive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on the technical merit of ideas, not the person presenting them.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and move forward.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or bad-faith participation.
- Publishing private information without explicit consent.
- Any behavior that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — GitHub issues, pull requests, comments, and any other communication directly related to the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the maintainer directly via GitHub. All reports will be reviewed and handled with discretion. The maintainer reserves the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions or comments that violate this code, and to temporarily or permanently ban participants.
